# Supporting reporting pages — draft design scope

Approved scope: compare supporting features with Eros while preserving the existing Platinum After Dark design. Use Eros's organisation as a reference, not its legal text, company details, fees, or operational promises.

## Implemented layouts

- Content complaints: content, access, conduct and billing issue categories.
- Copyright/photo complaints: ownership, likeness and consent concerns; no claim of an operational statutory notice channel.
- Safety reporting: serious-abuse categories, immediate-danger guidance, no media/document upload or monitored emergency claim.
- Legal requests: planned authority/identifier/scope fields and an outline of review subjects.
- Shared submission acknowledgement demonstration, explicitly stating nothing was submitted.
- Footer and related-channel navigation using the approved fabric, platinum borders, and typography.

## Before making these operational

Identify the actual operator and approved contacts; define monitored channels and responsible personnel; obtain applicable legal review; implement secure intake, access controls, retention and escalation; define complaint/copyright processes and any required designated-agent registration; test delivery and acknowledgement; publish truthful response procedures. Do not activate an unmonitored report form.

The prototype does not collect report contents, identity documents, or sensitive media. It has no promised response times or automatically adopted Eros provisions. Advertiser agreements remain outside the current solo-creator scope.
